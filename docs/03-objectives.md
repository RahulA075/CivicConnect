# CivicConnect — Objectives

## Primary Objectives

### O1 — Simplify Civic Issue Reporting
Provide citizens with a fast, intuitive mobile experience to report any civic issue in under 60 seconds, with photo evidence, geo-location tagging, and category selection.

**Success Metric:** 90% of reports submitted in under 60 seconds in usability testing.

---

### O2 — Eliminate Reporting Black Holes
Every submitted report receives an instant acknowledgment, a unique tracking ID, and automated status notifications at every stage of the resolution lifecycle.

**Success Metric:** 100% of reports receive an acknowledgment within 5 seconds of submission.

---

### O3 — Automate Issue Routing
Intelligently route submitted issues to the correct government department based on category, location, and jurisdiction — eliminating manual triage.

**Success Metric:** 95%+ of issues routed to the correct department without manual reassignment.

---

### O4 — Enable Real-Time Operations for Authorities
Provide government supervisors and field workers with a unified operations dashboard to view, assign, update, and close issues with full audit trail.

**Success Metric:** Supervisors can view all open issues in their jurisdiction in real time with zero data lag exceeding 30 seconds.

---

### O5 — Enforce SLA Compliance
Define configurable resolution SLAs per issue category and severity, automatically escalate overdue issues, and surface SLA metrics in authority dashboards.

**Success Metric:** SLA breach rate reduced by 40% within 6 months of deployment.

---

### O6 — Reduce Duplicate Reports
Implement location-based and category-based duplicate detection to surface existing open reports before a citizen submits, reducing noise for government departments.

**Success Metric:** Duplicate submission rate below 5% of total reports.

---

### O7 — Build a Citizen Feedback Loop
Allow citizens to confirm resolution quality, reopen incorrectly closed issues, and rate their experience — creating accountability for government performance.

**Success Metric:** 60%+ of resolved issues receive a citizen rating within 7 days.

---

### O8 — Deliver Actionable Analytics
Provide government planners and administrators with visual analytics — heatmaps, trend charts, category breakdowns, and department performance reports — to support data-driven infrastructure decisions.

**Success Metric:** At least 3 data-driven planning decisions documented per municipality per quarter as a result of platform insights.

---

### O9 — Ensure Inclusive Accessibility
Design the platform to support multiple languages, low-bandwidth environments, and screen readers, ensuring accessibility for citizens regardless of literacy, language, or device.

**Success Metric:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. Support for a minimum of 3 languages at launch.

---

### O10 — Build for Scale
Architect the backend and infrastructure to support multi-city, multi-department deployment with tenant isolation, without requiring code changes per city.

**Success Metric:** Onboard a new municipality in under 2 hours of configuration.

---

- AI-based issue classification (planned for v2.0)
