# CivicConnect — User Personas

---

## Persona 1 — The Engaged Citizen

**Name:** Priya Nair
**Age:** 34
**Occupation:** Software Engineer
**Location:** Urban metro area
**Device:** iPhone 14, high-speed internet

### Background
Priya is tech-savvy, civic-minded, and frustrated with the condition of roads in her neighborhood. She already uses social media to highlight issues but gets no structured response. She wants a proper channel where her report leads to measurable action.

### Goals
- Report issues quickly without filling out long forms
- Know that her report reached the right department
- Track resolution progress like she tracks a delivery
- Feel that her contribution made a difference

### Frustrations
- Calling hotlines and getting put on hold indefinitely
- Posting on social media and getting no official response
- Reporting the same pothole three times with no resolution

### Behaviors
- Submits 2–3 reports per month
- Checks status daily for active reports
- Rates resolved issues and leaves comments
- Upvotes existing reports she agrees with

### Platform Usage
- Primary: Mobile app
- Frequency: Weekly
- Feature affinity: Photo upload, real-time status, notifications, upvoting

## Persona 2 — The Low-Tech Citizen

**Name:** Ramesh Kumar
**Age:** 58
**Occupation:** Shopkeeper
**Location:** Semi-urban town
**Device:** Android entry-level phone, intermittent connectivity

### Background
Ramesh is not comfortable with complex apps. He has tried calling the local municipal office but gets routed endlessly. His shop entrance floods every monsoon because the drain outside is blocked. He wants to report it but technology intimidates him.

### Goals
- Report an issue without needing to understand technology
- Get a confirmation that someone will look into it
- Not have to repeat himself to multiple offices

### Frustrations
- Apps with too many steps and confusing menus
- No confirmation after calling in a complaint
- Language barrier — prefers his regional language

### Behaviors
- Would use the app if a family member helps him set it up
- Relies on push notifications to stay informed
- Likely to use voice features if available in future

### Platform Usage
- Primary: Mobile app (with simplified UI)
- Frequency: Occasional (event-driven)
- Feature affinity: Simple report flow, regional language support, SMS/push notifications

## Persona 3 — The Field Worker

**Name:** Suresh Patil
**Age:** 29
**Occupation:** Municipal Road Repair Crew Member
**Location:** Urban municipality
**Device:** Android mid-range phone, varies between WiFi and mobile data

### Background
Suresh receives work orders via WhatsApp from his supervisor, often with incomplete information about the location or nature of the issue. He completes repairs but has no way to officially close a ticket or upload proof of work. He wants clarity and recognition for completed work.

### Goals
- Receive clear, location-tagged task assignments
- Navigate directly to the issue site using maps
- Upload before/after photos to prove completion
- See his personal task completion record

### Frustrations
- Unclear or duplicate work orders via WhatsApp
- Driving to wrong locations due to bad addresses
- No system of record for work he has completed
- Supervisor not knowing work is done unless called

### Behaviors
- Checks assignments at start of each shift
- Updates status as he moves through each task
- Photographs completed work for records

### Platform Usage
- Primary: Mobile app (field worker interface)
- Frequency: Daily
- Feature affinity: Task list, map navigation, photo upload, status update

## Persona 4 — The Department Supervisor

**Name:** Anitha Rao
**Age:** 45
**Occupation:** Deputy Engineer, Water Supply Department
**Location:** City municipal office
**Device:** Desktop (work), Android (personal/mobile)

### Background
Anitha manages a team of 12 field workers responsible for all water-related civic issues across a ward. She currently tracks work through a combination of phone calls, spreadsheets, and physical registers. She is overwhelmed during monsoon season when issues spike.

### Goals
- See all open water issues across her ward in one place
- Assign and reassign tasks to field workers efficiently
- Identify overdue issues before they escalate to her superiors
- Reduce time spent on phone calls with field staff for status updates

### Frustrations
- Spreadsheet-based tracking is slow and error-prone
- No visibility into what field workers are doing at any moment
- Issues escalated to her superior before she knows they exist
- No easy way to report her team's performance upward

### Behaviors
- Logs in every morning to review overnight reports
- Reassigns issues if a field worker is absent
- Escalates complex issues requiring contractor involvement
- Reviews weekly summary reports with her team

### Platform Usage
- Primary: Web dashboard
- Frequency: Daily (multiple logins)
- Feature affinity: Issue queue, assignment interface, SLA alerts, workload view, status reports

## Persona 5 — The Municipal Administrator

**Name:** Commissioner D. Krishnaswamy
**Age:** 52
**Occupation:** Municipal Commissioner
**Location:** City headquarters
**Device:** Laptop (primary), iPad (secondary)

### Background
The Commissioner is responsible for the entire municipality's service delivery performance. He attends monthly council meetings where he is questioned on resolution rates and public satisfaction. He relies on manual reports from department heads, which are always delayed and sometimes inaccurate.

### Goals
- Get a real-time, accurate picture of service delivery across all departments
- Identify departments or wards that are consistently underperforming
- Use data to justify infrastructure budget requests
- Demonstrate to citizens and council members that governance is improving

### Frustrations
- Manual reports from departments take 2–3 weeks to compile
- Different departments use different formats — no unified view
- Reactive governance — he only learns of major issues after escalation
- No ability to benchmark performance over time

### Behaviors
- Reviews high-level dashboard weekly
- Drills into specific departments or wards when issues arise
- Exports reports for council presentations
- Sets quarterly performance targets for departments

### Platform Usage
- Primary: Web dashboard (analytics and overview)
- Frequency: Weekly deep review, daily glance
- Feature affinity: Executive dashboard, cross-department analytics, trend charts, export, SLA overview
